Privacy Policy

Updated: November 22, 2026

Text in your browser

Your idea, decisions and AI replies are saved in browser local storage. Clearing site data, changing devices or disabling storage can lose text. Export JSON at any time; legacy records are export-only.

What is sent to AI

Each inference sends the idea, decision, limited history and numerical state to DeepSeek. Our server does not save that text. DeepSeek processing and retention follow its own policy; we do not promise provider-side deletion or training exclusions. Avoid sensitive personal information.

Anonymous state and quota

Cloudflare D1 stores anonymous session hashes, numerical state, device time zone, daily eligibility, operation versions, model versions and token counts. Saves expire and are cleaned 30 days after successful progression, or creation for drafts. Daily eligibility resets at midnight in your device’s time zone. Quota metadata is retained for up to 180 days from the latest allocation renewal or first success.

Cookies and logs

An HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax cookie identifies an anonymous allocation, with Secure transport in production. It is not an advertising cookie. Short-window limits use HMAC-derived IP identifiers; the application database stores neither raw IPs nor simulation text. Cloudflare and DeepSeek may process necessary connection and operational logs.

Deletion and recovery

The delete control removes the server numerical save and operation records; quota remains until expiry. Clear browser storage separately to remove local text. Expiry, deletion or abandonment does not refund that local day’s used eligibility; a new daily eligibility is available at midnight in your device’s time zone. Local text cannot restore authoritative server state.

Optional external game

Silicon Valley Life Reboot loads deanchen.me only when you request it. That third-party site controls its code, connection logs and storage. Browser privacy settings may affect saves. We do not pass it your startup idea, decisions or save. Our deletion control does not manage external-site data. Use the original site and its own controls for that experience.

Public content and contact

Public pages may be indexed by search and AI crawlers. Private APIs are excluded from public content exports. This version has no accounts, checkout or email marketing. Report issues in the project GitHub repository without including secrets or simulation text.
