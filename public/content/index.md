Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers before you spend a minute or a dollar.

What counts as one simulation?

The first successful AI response uses the free eligibility. A normal run has six stages and at most 10 attempts. Failed attempts count, but eligibility is not used until the first success.

Do I need an account?

No. An anonymous cookie identifies a browser allocation, not a natural person. Clearing cookies or changing devices can create a new identity.

Where does my data live?

Text stays in the browser and is sent to DeepSeek for inference. The server retains anonymous numerical state for 30 days and quota metadata for 180 days.

Can I buy more simulations?

Not yet. Pro and Lifetime are future directions; their features and prices are unconfirmed.

Does deletion reset eligibility?

No. Deletion, abandonment or expiry does not refund used eligibility.

Are results reliable predictions?

They are assumption-based educational scenarios, not market research or success predictions.
