What the model calculates

AI proposes assumptions; code controls settlement and termination.

Cash and revenue

Paying customers times price gives monthly revenue. Interval net income and one-time costs update cash, which cannot fall below zero.

Runway

Cash divided by monthly net burn. If costs do not exceed revenue, the interface shows no net burn.

Model limits

The model omits taxes, receivables, financing and live market research. Team and commitment provide context rather than directly changing arithmetic.

Turn the scenario’s critical assumptions into real-world tests before committing resources.
